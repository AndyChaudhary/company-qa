\# \*\*Project Brief: Company Q\&A App\*\*



\### \*\*1. Problem Statement\*\*

Employees waste valuable time manually searching through scattered company documentation, emails, and guides to find operational answers (aligning with the hackathon's objective of building natural-language search over documents for real users \[1]).



\### \*\*2. Target Users\*\*

\* \*\*Onboarding Employees\*\*: Needing quick answers to company policies, tools, and setup steps.

\* \*\*Team Members \& Leads\*\*: Seeking fast retrieval of project guidelines, specifications, and organizational processes.



\### \*\*3. Three Most Useful Questions\*\*

1\. \*"What is our official company policy regarding \[specific process/benefit]?"\*

2\. \*"Where can I find the setup steps and requirements for \[internal tool/system]?"\*

3\. \*"Who is responsible for approving \[specific request or ticket]?"\*



\### \*\*4. What Makes It Trustworthy\*\*

\* \*\*Grounded Answers\*\*: Every generated response strictly relies on and cites internal source documents, preventing unverified claims \[2].

\* \*\*Evaluation \& Clear UX\*\*: Clear visual citations and source context in the user interface, paired with systematic answer evaluation \[2].

\* \*\*Technical Transparency\*\*: Full codebase transparency provided through a public GitHub repository detailing setup steps, system architecture, and test results \[2, 3].



\### \*\*5. Out of Scope\*\*

\* \*\*Ungrounded Generation\*\*: Answering queries outside uploaded company documents or making speculative claims without source backing \[2].

\* \*\*Autonomous Ticket/Workflow Action\*\*: Performing automated task execution or ticket resolution (which falls under the separate \*\*Agents\*\* and \*\*Automation\*\* hackathon tracks \[1, 3]).
